ClassWire is an independent Schoology companion for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It brings course activity, assignments, grades, messages, and learning resources into a native interface.

Stay on top of school
- See recent activity, comments, likes, and updates from your courses and groups.
- Review upcoming assignments, discussions, quizzes, and events.
- Browse courses, groups, school information, and supported parent-linked student accounts.

Manage coursework
- View course materials, pages, and nested resource folders.
- Review gradebook information, overall grades, feedback, and assignment submissions.
- Teachers can review student submissions and manage supported grades and rubric feedback when their Schoology permissions allow it.
- Submit supported text and file-based assignment work.

Communicate and collaborate
- Read and compose Schoology messages.
- Create supported updates, discussions, and events.
- Follow activity posts, read comments, reply, like posts, and participate in supported polls.
- View notifications and manage supported notification preferences.

Work with resources and files
- Browse personal, shared, and group resources.
- Create and edit supported resource items and folders when permitted.
- View and download attachments, preview documents and images, and play compatible audio and video using the system media player.

Optional AI assistance
- Ask the ClassWire AI assistant about supported Schoology courses, assignments, grades, materials, upcoming work, updates, and message threads.
- Choose from supported Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, and Puter provider options.
- Draft supported submissions, messages, updates, and teacher grade changes. ClassWire asks you to review and explicitly confirm supported actions before they are sent or saved.
- AI prompts and relevant Schoology context are sent to the AI provider you select. Review the privacy policy and provider terms before sharing education information.

ClassWire connects to your existing Schoology account. Available content and actions depend on the permissions and features enabled by your school and Schoology account. Some specialized Schoology content types and workflows may not be supported.
