# Privacy Policy for ClassWire

Last Updated: November 2026

ClassWire is engineered with student privacy as its core non-negotiable. This Privacy Policy details exactly how data is handled by our mobile client.

### 1. Zero Server Sourcing & Collection
ClassWire operates strictly as a local-first interface. 
* We do not host external web databases or backend servers.
* Your student grades, personal information, course rosters, and assignments are never transmitted to, processed by, or saved on any third-party infrastructure.
* We do not implement telemetry, user tracking pixel frameworks, or data analytics modules.

### 2. Sandbox Client Security
All authentication operations, session cookies, and API data payloads pulled from your Schoology endpoint are stored natively inside your device's secured client sandbox storage. Your information is isolated by the operating system and stays within the app.

### 3. Verification & Auditing
Because ClassWire is structured without backend data persistence, your security is tied directly to the encryption parameters maintained by your device and the official institutional authorization framework.
